1. Intro & Motivation
 Most real problems don't have closed form solutions, or if they do, they are unworkable or
dicult to interpret.

 Numerical approximations can be useful, but they can be expensive, they may have bad
numerical properties, or solutions may be inscrutable.

 Taylor series, etc. can be limited in applicability.
 Asymptotic methods emphasise usefulness, interpretability as well as accuracy.
